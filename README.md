# Masses and Spring – Oscillations and Spring Constant

## 📌 Project Overview

**Masses and Spring – Oscillations and Spring Constant** is an immersive VR-based physics experiment designed to help students understand the concepts of oscillatory motion and spring constant through interactive virtual experimentation.

The project recreates a physics laboratory experience in Virtual Reality, allowing users to interact with a mass-spring system, perform the experiment, observe oscillations, and record experimental observations in an immersive environment.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Understand mass-spring oscillations through interactive VR simulation.
- Explore the relationship between mass, displacement, and oscillatory motion.
- Demonstrate the principles behind spring constant.
- Provide an engaging alternative to traditional physics laboratory experiments.
- Enable students to learn through interaction, observation, and experimentation.

## 🧪 Experiment

The experiment focuses on studying the oscillatory motion of a mass attached to a spring and understanding the spring constant.

Users can attach a mass to the spring, pull it from its equilibrium position, release it, and observe its oscillatory motion.
